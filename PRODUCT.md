# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Recruiters and tech leads** evaluating Leonardo for a full-time (CLT) or contractor (PJ) role. They scan fast, often between other tabs, and need to judge seniority, scope of impact, and stack fit in seconds.
- **Freelance clients** deciding whether to hire him for a project or consultancy. They need confidence that he delivers in production, not just builds demos.

## Product Purpose

Personal site and portfolio of Leonardo Lopes, a software engineer and architect (web, backend, mobile) based in Brazil. Success is a visitor who leaves **convinced of his seniority and track record**. Contact (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, email, resume) is the natural next step, but the primary job is earning trust through experience.

## Positioning

A tech lead who has shipped measurable results in large, regulated, or high-volume production systems: healthtech AI diagnostics, national pricing for a global dairy group, B2B field sales for a top Brazilian beverage company, pharma, and edtech streaming. He covers frontend, backend (Node.js/TypeScript, Go), and mobile (React Native with native Kotlin bridges), plus infra (Terraform, OpenTelemetry, AWS).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: home, about, experiences, technologies, contact (WhatsApp form), styleguide (noindex).
- All content in English, inline in `src/utils/mock.ts`; no i18n yet.
- Resume link via `env.CV_URL`; contact via WhatsApp, LinkedIn, GitHub, Instagram, email.
- Next.js 16 + React 19 + Tailwind 4 + shadcn/ui (base-ui). Static, Cache Components.

## Brand Commitments

- Visual direction pinned by the owner: **neon on midnight**. Midnight `#030407`, cobalt `#1558c9`, saturated neon blue `#2f6bff`. Futuristic but current, neon, animated, frosted glass. NestJS's site is a reference for glow saturation.
- Must read as **professional**: a tech brand of a software engineer, not a gaming or crypto aesthetic.
- Voice: plain, direct, specific; no hype, no AI-sounding copy.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: every claim is backed by a role, a number, or a shipped system.
2. Respect the scan: a recruiter gets seniority, stack, and current role within one viewport.
3. Craft is the portfolio: the site itself demonstrates frontend quality and performance.
4. Contact is always one step away, never pushy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on the dark theme; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; full keyboard navigation.
